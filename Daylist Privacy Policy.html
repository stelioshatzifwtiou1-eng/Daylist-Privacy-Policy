1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Daylist (Demo) Android app (“Daylist”, “the app”), published by Stylianos Chatzifotiou, an individual developer based in Thessaloniki, Greece (“we”, “us”). Daylist is a daily habit and task tracker with a virtual companion, guided calm sessions, workouts and offline mini-games.

2. Information the app handles

Daylist does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The app stores the following information locally on your device:

InformationWhy it existsWhere it lives
Habit names, task names, due dates, priority flags and the dates you completed themTo show your lists, streaks and weekly statisticsOn your device only
XP, level, trophies, buddy form, buddy name, hats, themes, unlocks and mini-game resultsTo run progression and rewardsOn your device only

Anything you type into the app (for example a habit name or buddy name) stays on your device unless you choose to copy or share it yourself (see section 4). We do not operate a server that receives your Daylist data.

What we do not collect: personal identifiers, advertising ID, IMEI or other persistent device identifiers, precise or approximate location, health or fitness sensor data, contacts, SMS or call data, or the list of other apps on your device.

3. How we use information

The data stored on your device is used only to operate the app’s features: showing your habits and tasks, calculating streaks, XP and levels, scheduling the reminders you turn on, and personalising your buddy. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or research, and we do not combine it with data from other sources.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share your Daylist data with third parties. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. The following services are involved in the ways described:

5. Permissions

  • Internet access: used to load the app’s web font and, in future versions, optional online features (see section 10).
  • Notifications: used only for reminders and streak-rescue alerts that you enable. On Android 13 and later, the system asks for your permission first. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and are not sent from a server.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback in the app.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device’s system settings.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot normally access, and the app keeps a second copy in Android’s native preferences storage so that clearing the web cache does not erase your progress. Because the data never leaves your device through the app, there is no transmission to protect. If your device has Android backup enabled, the operating system may include app data in your device backup according to your device settings and Google’s terms. Protect your device with a screen lock to keep your data private.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. The app does not offer account creation, so there is no account to close. You can delete all Daylist data at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings (Apps, Daylist, Storage, Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s stored data from your device.

8. Children

Daylist is a general-audience productivity app and is not designed to collect information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or export on your behalf, and you control your data directly on your device. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have rights under data protection law, including the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. For data handled by Google (see section 4), please use Google’s own privacy tools. You may also contact us with any privacy question.

10. Future features

The demo mentions an online Arena and a global leaderboard that are planned for a future release and are not active in this version. Before any feature is enabled that sends data off your device, we will update this policy, describe exactly what is collected, why, and who receives it, and show a clear in-app notice and ask for your consent where required.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the store listing before they take effect.
